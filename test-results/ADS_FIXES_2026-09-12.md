## What changed

- Default cadence: one commercial opportunity after every two content videos in both Feed and Trending. Eighteen videos plan nine opportunities, including a possible final ad. Direct iAdMe creatives and Google native slots alternate under that one cadence; ads are not counted as content.
- Ads are added to the pager only after loading. Unfilled opportunities leave ordinary videos adjacent; no empty Sponsored page and no forced skip back onto the previous video.
- Stable placement keys and a shared pager preserve the visible content when loading, expiry, eviction or pagination changes indices. Structural changes wait for scrolling to settle. A regression test caught stale Flutter scroll extents after pagination; page selection now uses physical scroll position at that boundary.
- Demand preloads three upcoming native placements and the nearest previous one. A cache bounded to six native objects across both tabs, including at most two pending loads, retains recent ads for backward swipes. Each creative has one view owner; dispose follows view detachment.
- Failed requests retry automatically at 15, 30 and then 60 seconds. A 20-second app timeout cancels stuck requests and ignores late callbacks. Background/hidden views do not initiate new loads. Cache expiry at 55 minutes, long-background invalidation and memory-pressure handling prevent stale/unbounded inventory.
- Required privacy options remain accessible when ad SDK initialization fails; the final focused consent/cache suite passed **22 tests** including this additional regression.
- UMP refresh starts on launch; valid UMP saved consent can unblock ads during that refresh. Network/SDK errors are retryable instead of permanently cached. Native user consent forms are not timed out or bypassed in the app.
- Diagnostics include SDK code/domain/response ID, build/test inventory, counts and retry timing. Consent/SDK setup failures and missing production ad-unit configuration are distinguishable. Expected unfilled crossings are local counters, not a new error report per swipe.
- Release ID validation rejects blanks, app IDs, placeholders and Google sample units for production. `bash tool/build_store_release.sh android|ios --check` validates the corresponding environment variable without building; omitting `--check` builds a local store artifact and never deploys or uploads it. Correct ID shape does not verify account approval or fill.

## India relevance and serving limits

Native requests use the content keywords `India`, `local communities`, and `short videos`. They do not invent the user's location, send GPS/captions, or force UMP geography. Google describes keywords as contextual request information; the Flutter request API has no country/language override that guarantees an Indian creative. [Google Flutter AdRequest API](https://pub.dev/documentation/google_mobile_ads/latest/google_mobile_ads/AdRequest-class.html).

Development/staging/sample creatives are intentionally test inventory. Production uses a valid platform-specific real unit; no real inventory was used by the native test harness. [Google test ads](https://developers.google.com/admob/flutter/test-ads).

Country/language controls are available for publisher-managed AdMob campaigns. Account status, real ad-unit IDs, mediation availability, blocking controls and India country reporting must be reviewed in the actual account before claiming improved live fill/relevance. No such live configuration change is part of this local work. [AdMob campaign targeting](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6231370?hl=en).

The cache and consent choices follow Google's guidance to bound precached ads, avoid using cached creatives older than an hour, dispose after the view leaves the tree, and consult UMP before requests. [Native ad caching](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/native), [Flutter native views](https://developers.google.com/admob/flutter/native/platforms), [UMP lifecycle](https://developers.google.com/admob/flutter/privacy).

## Required owner acceptance before release

Use an affected older Android, a current Android and an iPhone with the same candidate build. In both Feed and Trending, swipe through at least 18 content videos, return across recent ad slots, scroll rapidly, switch tabs repeatedly, refresh/change feed scope, rotate the screen and background/resume. Compare available versus unfilled opportunities; when ads cannot load, content must remain uninterrupted. Repeat with poor/offline connectivity, consent denied then allowed, and a long background period. Verify native creatives actually render and do not leave blank platform views.

Use official test units for automated/dev checks. Live fill, Indian creative relevance and device/vendor-specific behavior require a separately authorized candidate release and account/device review. Do not infer nine paid impressions merely from nine planned slots.
